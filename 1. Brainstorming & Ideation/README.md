# Phase 1 – Brainstorming and Ideation

## Project Title

LegalEase – AI Powered Legal Document Generator

## Problem Statement

Creating legal documents manually can be difficult and time-consuming.
Many people do not know the correct format of legal documents and may
find legal terminology difficult to understand.

## Proposed Solution

LegalEase is an AI-powered application that helps users generate basic
legal document drafts by collecting the required information through
a simple and user-friendly interface.

## Objectives

1. Simplify the process of creating basic legal documents.
2. Reduce the time required to prepare document drafts.
3. Provide an easy-to-use interface.
4. Generate structured legal document drafts.
5. Allow users to download the generated document.

## Target Users

- Students
- Individuals
- Small businesses
- People who need basic legal document drafts

## Key Features

- User-friendly interface
- Legal document type selection
- User information input
- AI-assisted document generation
- Document preview
- Download option

## Expected Outcome

The project aims to develop a simple AI-powered application that helps
users generate structured legal document drafts based on the information
provided by them.
